Support

Help for Maven Reader, a simple ebook reader for PDFs and EPUBs.

How to use

  • Add PDF or EPUB files to your library.
  • Open a book and read offline anytime.
  • Progress and preferences are saved as you use the app.

Need help?

Email info@maven-stories.com and include the app name, your device model, and OS version if something isn’t working.
