Support

Help for Maven Stories on iPhone and iPad.

How to use

  • Browse or search for stories from the home screen.
  • Open a story to read, or play audio when available.
  • Sign in to sync library progress across devices, where offered.
  • Progress and preferences are saved as you use the app.

Need help?

Email support@maven-stories.com and include the app name, your device model, and iOS version if something isn’t working.
